# Product vision

Shewiun CFA Business turns company context into useful, reviewable work with specialised agents. Its primary customers are small software and AI agencies, consultants, founders and professional teams. The existing application is the foundation.

The product hypothesis is a Project-to-Deliverable Workspace: brief → research → specialist contribution → draft → review → approval → deliverable. Users should receive a saved, editable result linked to their project, with evidence, assumptions and review history where appropriate. Select one primary workflow with design partners before implementing several shallow variants.

Freedom, intelligence and control belong together: users create specialised agents; agents collaborate on useful work; deterministic backend permissions and policies govern execution. The Agent Control Plane owns identity, registry, grants, risk, approvals and evidence. The runtime executes authorized steps. Agents cannot grant themselves authority or bypass review.

This is a differentiation hypothesis, not proof that Shewiun outperforms CrewAI, Grok Bot or another product. Approvals, memory, multiple models and audit logs are established capabilities. Evaluate the complete customer outcome instead of counting features.

Shewiun Personal is a future distinct experience on the shared core, not Business Free. It is mobile-first with a full desktop experience and includes coding, research, learning, writing and personal planning. Fitness and nutrition belong only in Personal and require appropriate safety boundaries. Personal and organisational data must remain isolated.

Business remains desktop-first and fully usable on phones and tablets. Personal and Business should reuse components and data access rather than duplicate applications. Good typography, clear hierarchy, breathing room, visible status and honest capability labels matter more than a dense dashboard.

Current scope follows the engineering brief: Batch 0 audit, then Batch 1 responsive UX and a clearly labelled interactive landing demo. No account split, desktop app or production migration/deployment in these batches. The long-term mission is recorded in `PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md`; completed capabilities are in `FEATURE_STATUS.md`.
